# Emoji Credits

`emoji.json` is generated from two Unicode data files, so the emoji picker can
list, group and search emojis without a network lookup.

## Unicode emoji-test.txt, Emoji 18.0

**License:** Unicode License v3, [unicode.org/Public/emoji/latest/emoji-test.txt](https://unicode.org/Public/emoji/latest/emoji-test.txt)

Codepoints, names and groups. Only fully-qualified entries, without skin tone
and hair variants.

## CLDR annotations, en

**License:** Unicode License v3, [github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json](https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json)

Search keywords, from `cldr-annotations-full` and
`cldr-annotations-derived-full`, `annotations/en`.

## Emoji images

The emojis themselves are drawn with the system's colour emoji font.
